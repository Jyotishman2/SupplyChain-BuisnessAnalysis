# Supply Chain & Inventory Intelligence Platform

## 1. Business Scenario

NexaMart is a large e-commerce and retail company that sells
consumer products across India.

The company operates multiple warehouses and works with hundreds
of suppliers to fulfill millions of customer orders.

The objective of this project is to build a Supply Chain &
Inventory Intelligence Platform that provides visibility into
inventory, suppliers, warehouses, orders, shipments, deliveries,
and returns.

---

## 2. Business Problem

NexaMart currently faces several supply-chain challenges:

- Stock-outs of high-demand products
- Excess inventory of slow-moving products
- Uneven inventory distribution across warehouses
- Supplier delivery delays
- Late customer deliveries
- Order cancellations
- High product return rates
- Increasing inventory carrying costs
- Difficulty identifying poor-performing suppliers
- Inaccurate demand forecasts

The company needs a centralized analytics platform to identify
these problems and support data-driven decisions.

---

## 3. Business Objectives

The platform should help the business:

1. Reduce product stock-outs.
2. Reduce excess and slow-moving inventory.
3. Improve inventory turnover.
4. Improve supplier performance.
5. Improve warehouse utilization.
6. Increase customer order fill rate.
7. Improve on-time delivery.
8. Reduce order cycle time.
9. Reduce unnecessary inventory carrying costs.
10. Improve demand forecast accuracy.
11. Understand product return patterns.

---

## 4. Key Business Questions

### Inventory

- Which products are frequently out of stock?
- Which products are overstocked?
- Which products are slow-moving?
- Which products have the highest inventory turnover?
- Which warehouses have excess inventory?
- Which warehouses are experiencing stock-outs?

### Suppliers

- Which suppliers consistently deliver late?
- Which suppliers have poor fill rates?
- Which suppliers have the longest lead times?
- Which suppliers contribute most to delays?

### Warehouses

- Which warehouses are performing best?
- Which warehouses have inventory imbalance?
- Which warehouses fulfill the most orders?
- Where should inventory be repositioned?

### Orders & Delivery

- What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
- What percentage of orders are cancelled?
- What is the average order cycle time?
- Which products or regions experience the most delays?

### Returns

- What products have the highest return rates?
- Why are customers returning products?
- Which suppliers are associated with defective products?
- What is the financial impact of returns?

---

## 5. Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Inventory Turnover | Measure inventory efficiency |
| Stock-out Rate | Measure product availability |
| Fill Rate | Measure order fulfillment |
| Supplier On-Time % | Measure supplier reliability |
| Supplier Fill Rate | Measure supplier fulfillment |
| On-Time Delivery % | Measure customer delivery performance |
| Order Cycle Time | Measure order processing speed |
| Carrying Cost | Measure cost of holding inventory |
| Return Rate | Measure product/customer issues |
| Forecast Accuracy | Measure demand prediction quality |

---

## 6. Intentional Data Problems

The synthetic dataset will contain realistic business scenarios:

- Seasonal demand
- Fast-moving products
- Slow-moving products
- Supplier delays
- Warehouse inventory imbalance
- Stock-outs
- Overstock
- Cancelled orders
- Late deliveries
- Product returns

These scenarios will allow the analytics platform to demonstrate
real-world supply-chain problems.

---

## 7. Expected Business Outcome

The final platform should allow supply-chain managers to quickly
identify operational problems and answer:

"Where is the supply chain failing, why is it failing, and what
should we investigate?"

The final analytics solution will provide dashboards and KPIs
for inventory, suppliers, warehouses, orders, deliveries, and
returns.
